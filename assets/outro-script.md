# Video Outro Script Template

Use this template to close each episode with a consistent sign-off while encouraging community engagement.

---

## Standard Closing

> "Big thanks to the Harper engineering team—especially Kyle and Kris—for building a platform that's this much fun to explore. That's it for this week. There's fast, then there's Harper fast. Until next Friday."

---

## Community Engagement (Optional Add-on)

Include one or more of these elements before the standard closing when appropriate:

**General Engagement:**
> "If you have questions about what we covered today, drop them in the comments or join us on Discord—I'd love to hear what you're working on."

**Topic Request:**
> "What should I tackle next week? Let me know in the comments or submit a topic request on GitHub."

**Feedback Request:**
> "This is [week number], and I'm still figuring out the best way to present these concepts. Your feedback helps shape the series—let me know what's working."

**Challenge/Call to Action:**
> "Now it's your turn—try [specific action from episode] and share your results in the Discord. I'm curious to see what you build."

**Preview Next Week:**
> "Next week, we're diving into [topic]—it builds directly on what we covered today, so make sure you've got [prerequisite] set up."

---

## Customization Notes

- **Core closing:** Keep consistent for brand recognition and clean exit
- **Engagement:** Rotate different calls-to-action to avoid repetition
- **Tone:** Confident but inviting—you're a fellow learner, not a lecturer
- **Duration:** 15-30 seconds including any engagement element
- **Links:** Mention Discord and GitHub naturally without being pushy
