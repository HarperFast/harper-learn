# Video Intro Script Template

Use this template as a starting point for each episode's introduction. Adapt the specifics to match the week's topic while maintaining a consistent voice and structure.

---

## Standard Opening

> "I'm Ivan Judson, Solution Architect at Harper. I spend my days helping customers understand how to apply our platform to their toughest problems. These Friday sessions are my way of sharing what I'm learning—so you can see how Harper solves real challenges.
>
> Pull up a chair—let's talk about what I've been building this week."

---

## Episode-Specific Hook

**Week 01 Example:**
> "This week, I'm starting from scratch—installing Harper for the first time and taking my first steps with the platform. If you've been curious about Harper but weren't sure where to begin, this is the episode for you."

---

## Customization Notes

- **Opening line:** Keep consistent across all episodes to establish brand recognition
- **Hook:** Tailor to the specific topic and what viewers will learn
- **Tone:** Professional but conversational—like a colleague sharing discoveries over coffee
- **Duration:** Aim for 30-45 seconds total
- **Energy:** Welcoming and enthusiastic without being over-the-top

---

## Alternative Hooks by Topic Type

**Installation/Getting Started:**
> "Today we're starting at the very beginning—getting Harper up and running on your machine."

**Deep Technical Dive:**
> "This week, we're going under the hood to understand how [specific feature] really works."

**Practical Project:**
> "I've got a real-world problem to solve this week, and Harper might be exactly what we need."

**Troubleshooting/Debugging:**
> "Ever run into [common problem]? This week, I'm diving into how to debug and resolve it."

**Performance/Optimization:**
> "Speed matters. This week, we're exploring how to make Harper work even faster."

---

*Keep it genuine, keep it focused, and let your curiosity show.*
